What's what?

Big moths are your sessions, small ones their agents, and mid-sized ones circling another moth are side sessions it started. The flame burns brighter the more are working.

A moth that needs you lands on the lantern and turns indigo, and the light goes violet. One that's waiting on its agents keeps flying.

Hover a moth for its card. Click it (or its card) to bring its terminal tab, or its session in the Claude app, to the front. Right-click to snooze it until it does something new, again to snooze it for good, and once more to wake it.

Fireflies are a session's PRs:readyfailingchangesdraftin review. Hover a cluster for the list.

The wisps drifting low are your open PRs that no session owns and nobody has touched for a week, the oldest lowest. Hover one for what it needs, click to open it. Touch it again and it rises away. A faint ghost moth behind them is an old session that made some: click it to resume that session in a new iTerm tab.

Threads pulse when a session runs a tool. A grey moth hit an API error. Landed moths get restless after 15 minutes.

The chart button opens your stats: how much of your moths' time was warm today, the day as a ribbon, the week before it, goals and badges. Hover or tap a badge to see what it is.
