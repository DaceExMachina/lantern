ribbon
Colours the bookmark ribbon that hangs inside the barrel lantern. The other lanterns have no ribbon, so these are greyed out on them.
quiet from
Turns quiet on by itself every night at this time, even with no window open. It snoozes every session until quiet hours end.
quiet until
Sets when quiet hours end and moths can call you again. Switch it off and quiet lasts until you press the moon. Sets when one day ends and the next begins in your stats, badges and recap. Set it later than midnight to keep late nights with the day they belong to.
What's what?

Big moths are your sessions and small ones are their agents. The more of them working, the brighter the flame.

When a session needs you, its moth lands on the lantern and the light turns violet.

Hover a moth for its card. Click to jump to its session, or shift-click or right-click to snooze it. Do it again and it sleeps, snoozed until you wake it.

Fireflies are its PRs:readyfailingchangesdraftin review. The pale wisps drifting low are stale PRs nobody owns.

The chart button opens your stats, and the moon snoozes everything until 8am. Set quiet from to have it come on by itself each night.

The day rollover is when a new day starts in your stats. Set it past midnight to keep late nights with the day before.
